A LITTLE CONTEXT

My path has been technical.
My perspective got broader.

I started out building full-stack products — working across interfaces, backend services, databases and integrations. As the companies and systems around me grew, I became increasingly involved in the problems behind the software: payment flows, data integrity, operational tooling and incidents that needed a practical fix.

That experience taught me to look beyond a ticket. I like understanding why a problem matters, tracing it across systems, choosing a solution that can survive real-world edge cases and working with the people who use it.

My most recent role included a lot of technical operations and cross-functional ownership, so my title doesn’t tell the whole story of my engineering background. I’m now looking for a hands-on role where I can build again, deepen my technical craft and bring that broader perspective to the team.
