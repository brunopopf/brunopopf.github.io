PAYMENTS · STRIPE · MIGRATION01

Migrating payment methods without disrupting recurring revenue.

A multi-market migration of more than 2,000 active payment identifiers required more than moving data. The challenge was maintaining data integrity and continuity across existing payment workflows.

I worked on the migration involving Stripe payment infrastructure, handling technical edge cases and coordinating the work needed to keep recurring payment flows reliable.

The migration covered payment methods associated with approximately 80% of recurring revenue.
